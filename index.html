I make AI that actually gets used.

I'm Mugu, an AI/ML engineer and grad student at the University of Houston. At TMAC I built DeltaZero, an LLM pipeline that checks a manufacturer's orders against their ERP before mistakes ship. Order-entry errors dropped from about 20% to under 1% on evaluated samples, going after roughly $100K a year in logistics losses.

At HPE I built ReVolve, a digital twin of their reverse supply chain. It showed a 15%+ throughput gain before anyone changed a real process, and HPE is now extending it to their Mexico facility.

So what? I'm the engineer you bring in when a demo has to become something people use every day, with numbers the business can check. I've published two peer-reviewed NLP papers, but I judge my work by whether it's still running months later.

Outside work: guitar, skateboarding, and a lot of food.
